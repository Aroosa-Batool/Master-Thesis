03

Shared scenario

Imagine that you own a small desktop social robot and wear its connected smartwatch. The robot is about to deliver a sensitive reminder while another person might be nearby.

Before speaking, the robot checks whether a bystander is present. If it detects one, it privately asks on your watch whether the reminder may be spoken aloud. If you say no or do not answer, the reminder appears privately on your watch.

In every condition, sensing is processed locally and raw audio and video are not retained. For this scenario, the robot stores a compact face or voice representation and your previous yes/no choice locally so it can recognize a returning bystander and reuse your choice. Sensor status is visible.

The images are standardized AI-generated illustrations of hypothetical conditions, not photographs of a real participant or actual sensor recordings.
